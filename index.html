About the company

A focused real-estate business in Uttar Pradesh

Rich Life Real Revolution Pvt. Ltd. describes its work in the residential and commercial land sectors, with a stated focus on customer needs, transparency and responding to market trends. This concept keeps that positioning but presents it more clearly.

  • Residential and commercial plotted development projects.
  • Head office in Phaphamau and a branch office in Civil Lines, Prayagraj.
  • Project information is presented with source links and confirmation notes.

This concept does not claim founding year, completed-project counts, awards, customer numbers, RERA registration or years of experience. Those details must be supplied and verified by the company before being published.
